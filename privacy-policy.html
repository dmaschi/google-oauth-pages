Privacy policy — mcpGmail

Last updated: October 5, 2026

mcpGmail is a personal Google Workspace integration operated by Dario Maschi. This policy also covers the Google Tasks Desktop component using the same Google OAuth project.

Information accessed and its purpose

Depending on granted permissions and the requested workflow, the tools access Google account data such as email messages and attachments, files, calendar events, document contents, spreadsheets, presentations, forms and responses, tasks, contacts, chat messages, and Apps Script projects. This information is used to carry out the account owner's workflows, including reading, searching, organizing, drafting, and authorized updates. The desktop Tasks component uses task-list and task information to display and manage tasks.

Processing and sharing

Data is exchanged with Google's APIs and processed on computers and services controlled by the account owner. When the owner uses a connected software assistant, the Google information retrieved for that workflow may be provided to that assistant and its configured AI service provider for processing. Those providers' applicable privacy and retention policies also apply. The integration does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, and does not use Google user data to develop or train generalized AI models.

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored separately from public source files in protected credential storage on the owner's machines or services. Downloaded files, logs, cached information, and assistant conversation history may be retained as needed for the owner's workflows. Retention depends on the configured application or assistant; this integration does not promise automatic deletion after a fixed period. Credentials and private account data are not published on this website.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API access but does not automatically erase previously downloaded data or assistant history. Those copies can be removed from the corresponding local files, application storage, or assistant service.

This website

These public information pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no Google sign-in form, advertising, or application-added analytics. GitHub may process visitor connection information under its own privacy statement.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact dario.maschi@gmail.com.
